How it works

Four steps from skills to spreadsheet

Most job boards make you search manually, click through pages of irrelevant listings, and repeat the same process every day. LetsJobify automates the crawl, the filter, and the export so you can focus on preparing applications for roles that actually fit your profile.

1

Describe your profile

Enter your skills (e.g. React, Node.js, Kubernetes), your preferred work location type, and your sector. That is all we need to get started.

2

We search 9 boards at once

LetsJobify queries RemoteOK, Remotive, Jobicy, WeWorkRemotely, EU Remote Jobs, Himalayas, Arbeitnow, 4dayweek, and Remote.co simultaneously in the background.

3

Results are filtered and deduplicated

We match your skills against job titles, tags, and descriptions. Duplicate listings that appear on multiple boards are automatically removed.

4

Download your shortlist

A clean Excel file lands in your browser within seconds. Each row has the job title, company, description, posting date, and a direct link to the original listing.

Our sources include general remote boards, EU-focused platforms, and niche boards for four-day-week and visa-sponsored roles. Together they cover a broad range of remote engineering and IT positions across North America, Europe, and the Asia-Pacific region.

Why use LetsJobify

Less searching, more applying

General job boards like LinkedIn and Indeed are comprehensive but noisy. A search for "Python engineer remote" can return thousands of listings — many of which are poorly matched, out of date, or not genuinely remote. Filtering through them manually takes hours.

LetsJobify is scoped narrowly: it focuses on remote and engineering roles, applies skill-based filtering before you ever see a result, and delivers the output in a format that is easy to track and share. It is not a replacement for LinkedIn — it is a faster starting point for engineers who know what stack they want to work in.
